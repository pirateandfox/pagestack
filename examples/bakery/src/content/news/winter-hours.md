From the first Sunday in November we'll open at 8 a.m. and close at 2 p.m. on Sundays, so the bakers get a little more daylight. Wednesday to Saturday stay the same.

## The holiday table

The holiday order list opens on **November 1st**. This year:

1. Panettone, with candied orange and dark chocolate
2. Stollen, with a marzipan centre
3. Our fig and walnut loaf, in a festive box

Orders close on December 18th or when the oven is full, whichever comes first. Ask at the counter or email us.
