Every year someone asks in October, and every year we say "not yet". This year we gave in early.

Our hot cross buns are made with the same dough as the morning buns, enriched with milk and butter, and studded with currants and candied orange. We peel and candy the orange ourselves on Thursday afternoons, which is why the whole shop smells of marmalade.

## When to come

- **Fridays only**, from 7 a.m.
- About sixty a week, so the early birds do well.
- You can **order ahead** by Thursday at 5 p.m. for a box of six.

> A warm one, split and buttered, with a flat white. That's the whole plan.

See you Friday.
