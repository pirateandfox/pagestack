Every loaf of bread we sell begins with Gus, a jar of flour and water that Maya started in a flat kitchen in 2015.

Gus is fed twice a day, at 6 a.m. and 6 p.m., with equal weights of water and stone-ground flour from Two Rivers Mill. In summer he's lively by lunchtime; in winter he sulks and we move him nearer the oven.

## Take a bit home

If you'd like to bake at home, bring a clean jar on any Saturday and we'll give you some of Gus for free. He comes with a card of instructions and our phone number, in case he stops bubbling.

People have taken Gus to four continents so far. One of his descendants lives on a sailing boat.
